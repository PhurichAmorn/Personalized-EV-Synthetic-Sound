EV Sound Map

Every dot is a generated sound that passed the guardrail (sharpness, roughness, pulsing, crowding) and sounds different from all the others. Click one to hear it on a 22 s simulated drive (launch, climb to 110 km/h, ease off, coast), then mark any that sound bad.

Octave layers (Shepard-like, normal behaviour)

The same note in several octaves, each octave with its own texture and its own slow swell, played by the app's granular player like any other sound: pitch rises with speed, holds at cruise, falls on regen. A fixed low peak after the player stays put while the pitch climbs, so it stays deep. No endless rise and no octave jumps.
